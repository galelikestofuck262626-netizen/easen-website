About EASEN

A furniture maker for the hospitality industry.

Founded in 2016 in Lecong, Foshan — the heart of China's furniture industry — EASEN builds commercial furniture for restaurants, cafés, hotels and outdoor dining.

Who we are

We don't just sell furniture. We help you get the room right.

EASEN was founded in 2016 in Lecong, Foshan. Our 10,000 m² production base handles frames, upholstery, finishing and export packing, and our catalogue now covers more than 330 designs for restaurants, cafés, hotels and outdoor dining.

Most of our work is made to order. Designers and operators send us drawings, references or a single photo; we develop the pieces, confirm them with samples and drawings, and build them to specification.

What we care about is simple: that the pieces fit the room, match the sample, survive service — and arrive when you need them.

Quality & compliance

Built for commercial use, documented for your specifier.

Learn more

01Fire-safety options

Upholstery, foam and constructions available to EN 1021-1/2, BS 5852, CAL TB 117-2013 and NFPA 260 requirements.

02Commercial-grade construction

Reinforced timber or steel frames, high-density foam and contract fabrics for high-traffic dining rooms.

03Low-emission materials

Low-VOC, low-formaldehyde options including E0/E1 panels and CARB / TSCA Title VI compliant plywood.

04Responsible timber

FSC / PEFC certified timber and chain-of-custody documents can be arranged for European and international projects.

05Seven-step QC

Raw material, frame, upholstery, dimension, colour and packing checks, closing with photos of your actual pieces.

06Documents on request

Test reports and certificates are supplied per project and per material — ask us for what your specification requires.
