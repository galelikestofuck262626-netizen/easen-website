Jakarta Rooftop Garden Restaurant Furniture Project

المشروع

A rooftop garden restaurant in Jakarta, Indonesia, approx. 400 sqm (terrace and semi-outdoor dining), positioned as a lush, naturally airy tropical garden dining space. Warm terracotta floors, curved timber-batten ceilings, large glazing and abundant greenery create a natural, relaxed and warm dining atmosphere.

Client needs: furniture must match the tropical garden tone and be weather-resistant; semi-outdoor and terrace zones must be moisture- and sun-proof with durable materials; wood chairs must be comfortable and easy to clean; indoor and outdoor styles must transition seamlessly; furniture must resist mould in a planted environment.

Solution

Solid wood and rattan as main materials echoing the garden; rot-treated wood chairs with weatherproof tabletops in semi-outdoor zones; solid-wood tables and chairs by the glazing to draw the view in; quick-dry moisture-resistant cushions; metal parts anti-corrosion treated.

Result

Wood and rattan chairs with timber-batten ceilings, greenery and glazing form a natural, airy garden atmosphere; treated furniture suits tropical heat and humidity; indoor and outdoor transition seamlessly; quick-dry cushions ease rainy-season upkeep; the whole balances a natural experience with commercial durability.
