Bar Stool Options

Bar stools are the most critical piece of bar furniture — they must be comfortable enough for hours of sitting, stable enough to prevent tipping, and durable enough to withstand nightly use. EASEN manufactures all common bar stool types:

Backless Bar Stools

Space-saving, tuck fully under bar, clean sightlines across the venue.

Best for:

  • Crowded bars where guests stand and sit frequently
  • Perimeter bar seating along walls or windows
  • Maximizing seating capacity in tight spaces

Typical heights: 24" (counter), 28-30" (bar height)

Materials: Wood seat + metal base, fully metal, upholstered cushion

Low-Back Bar Stools

Short backrest (6-10" tall) provides lumbar support without blocking views or conversation.

Best for:

  • Restaurant bars where guests dine at the bar
  • Breweries and taprooms with 1-2 hour average stays
  • Hotel lobby bars and lounges

Comfort level: Good for 1-2 hour stays

Popular styles: Industrial metal frame, mid-century modern wood, upholstered

Full-Back Bar Stools

High backrest (12-18" tall) provides full back support for extended sitting.

Best for:

  • Fine dining restaurant bars
  • Hotel bars with table service
  • Upscale cocktail lounges
  • Casino bars and gaming lounges

Comfort level: Excellent for 2+ hour stays

Trade-off: More visual weight; requires more space per stool

Swivel Bar Stools

360° rotating seat allows easy entry/exit and conversation in multiple directions.

Pros:

  • Easier for guests to get on/off (no need to pull stool out)
  • More flexible seating — turn toward bar or away to socialize
  • Premium feel

Cons:

  • More expensive (swivel mechanism adds $15-30 per stool)
  • Higher maintenance (swivel bearing can wear out)

Mechanism: Ball-bearing swivel plate; 300-500 lb weight capacity

Adjustable-Height Bar Stools

Gas lift mechanism allows seat height adjustment (typically 22-30" range).

Best for:

  • Venues with mixed bar heights (counter + bar height)
  • Home bar and residential customers
  • Modern/contemporary aesthetics

Trade-off: Gas lifts are less durable than fixed-height stools in heavy commercial use; typically 3-5 year lifespan in high-traffic bars

Saddle & Tractor Bar Stools

Contoured seat shaped like a saddle or tractor seat — unique aesthetic and ergonomic design.

Best for:

  • Rustic or industrial-themed bars
  • Steakhouse and BBQ restaurant bars
  • Brewery taprooms

Materials: Solid wood or molded plywood seat; metal base

Bar Stool Materials

Wood Bar Stools

  • Wood species: Beech (most common), oak, walnut, ash, rubberwood
  • Finish: Natural stain + clear coat, painted (black, white, colors), distressed/weathered
  • Construction: Solid wood or plywood seat; mortise-and-tenon joinery for legs
  • Durability: Excellent; can be refinished
  • Aesthetic: Warm, traditional, rustic, or mid-century modern

Metal Bar Stools

  • Materials: Steel tube frame (most common), aluminum, wrought iron
  • Finish: Powder-coat (black, white, colors, metallic), brushed steel, antique bronze
  • Construction: Welded steel frame; 16-18 gauge steel for commercial use
  • Durability: Extremely durable; powder-coat finish resists scratches and rust
  • Aesthetic: Industrial, modern, minimalist
  • Weight capacity: 400-500 lbs (higher than wood stools)

Upholstered Bar Stools

  • Frame: Wood or metal frame with padded seat (and back if applicable)
  • Upholstery: Commercial vinyl (most durable), performance fabric, leather
  • Foam: High-density foam (2.0-2.5 lb/ft³ for commercial use)
  • Durability: Vinyl rated for 100,000+ double rubs (Wyzenbeek test)
  • Best for: Fine dining, hotel bars, upscale cocktail lounges
  • Maintenance: Wipe-clean vinyl; fabric requires periodic steam cleaning

Bar Tables

Bar-height tables (40-42" tall) create standing/perching zones and increase capacity beyond fixed bar seating. EASEN manufactures bar tables in all configurations:

Bar Table Types

Round Bar Tables

  • Sizes: 24"Ø, 30"Ø, 36"Ø, 42"Ø
  • Seating: 2-4 guests per table
  • Best for: Cocktail bars, nightclubs, informal standing/perching
  • Base: Single pedestal (most common), tripod, 4-leg

Square Bar Tables

  • Sizes: 24"x24", 30"x30", 36"x36", 42"x42"
  • Seating: 2-4 guests per table
  • Best for: Space-efficient layouts; fit into corners and along walls
  • Base: Single pedestal, 4-leg

Rectangular Bar Tables

  • Sizes: 24"x42", 30"x48", 30"x60", 30"x72"
  • Seating: 4-8 guests
  • Best for: Groups, communal seating in breweries and taprooms
  • Base: Two pedestals, trestle base, 4-leg

Wall-Mounted Bar Shelves

  • Sizes: 12-18" deep x 24-96" wide
  • Function: Narrow counter along walls with bar stools
  • Best for: Maximizing capacity in small spaces, window seating
  • Mount: Wall brackets or corbels; 50-100 lbs per linear foot capacity

Bar Table Bases

  • Cast iron pedestal: Heavy, stable, classic look; 50-80 lbs for 30" table
  • Steel column pedestal: Modern, lighter than cast iron; powder-coat finish
  • X-base or cross-base: Four legs extending from center; very stable
  • Tripod base: Three legs; works well on uneven floors
  • Four-leg bases: Traditional; best for rectangular tables

Base weight rule: Base should be at least 25-30% of table top weight for stability. Add weight plates to prevent tipping in high-traffic venues.

Bar Booth & Banquette Seating

Built-in booth seating creates defined zones, increases capacity, and adds comfortable seating options beyond bar stools. EASEN manufactures custom booth seating for bars and nightclubs:

Booth Seating Types for Bars

Single-Sided Banquettes

Upholstered bench along walls with bar tables or regular tables in front.

Dimensions: 18-24" seat depth x any length

Seat height: 18" for standard table, 24-26" for bar-height table

Best for: Perimeter seating, maximizing capacity along walls

Back-to-Back Booths

Two booths sharing a divider — creates intimate seating zones in center of room.

Common sizes: 44-48" total width (two 18-20" seats + 6-8" divider)

Best for: Restaurant bars, hotel bars, fine dining bar areas

Curved Booths

Semi-circular or C-shaped booths — premium VIP seating.

Capacity: 4-8 guests per booth

Best for: Nightclubs, VIP lounges, bottle service areas

Cost: More expensive due to curved frame fabrication

High-Back Booths

Extra-tall backrests (48-60" tall) create privacy and visual drama.

Best for: Nightclub VIP sections, speakeasy bars, luxury hotel bars

Acoustic benefit: Tall backs reduce noise bleed between booths

Booth Upholstery & Finishes

  • Vinyl upholstery: Most durable for bars (easy to wipe clean after spills); 100,000+ double rub rating; hundreds of colors
  • Tufted upholstery: Button-tufted or diamond-tufted backs — classic upscale look; adds visual interest
  • Leather upholstery: Premium option for hotel bars and upscale lounges; requires more maintenance
  • Channel-back design: Vertical channels in backrest — modern, sleek aesthetic
  • Wood trim: Wood cap rail on top of booth back — adds warmth and protects upholstery edge
  • Metal accents: Brass or stainless steel nail heads, metal divider panels — industrial or Art Deco styles

Bar Furniture FAQ

What is the typical lead time for custom bar furniture?

Standard production: 6-8 weeks from order confirmation to ex-factory
With pre-production sample: 2-3 weeks for sample + 6-8 weeks for production = 8-11 weeks total
Rush orders: 4-5 week expedited production available for certain designs (surcharge applies)
Shipping: Add 4-6 weeks for sea freight (most economical) or 7-10 days for air freight

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ varies by furniture type:

  • Bar stools: 50 stools per style/finish for wood; 30 stools for upholstered
  • Bar tables: 20 tables per size/finish combination
  • Booth seating: 100 linear feet minimum (typically 4-6 booth sections)
  • Complete bar packages: For full venue orders (stools + tables + booths), we can accommodate lower per-item MOQs

How do I choose between swivel and stationary bar stools?

Choose swivel stools when:

  • Comfort and ease of entry/exit are priorities (fine dining, hotel bars)
  • You want a premium feel
  • Bar is wide enough that guests don't need to pull stool out to sit

Choose stationary stools when:

  • Cost is a concern (save $15-30 per stool)
  • Durability is top priority (no moving parts to wear out)
  • High-traffic, casual environment (sports bars, breweries)

What weight capacity should bar stools have?

Commercial bar stools should support 300-500 lbs depending on construction:

  • Wood bar stools: 300-400 lbs typical
  • Metal bar stools: 400-500 lbs (steel frame with reinforced welds)
  • Upholstered bar stools: 350-450 lbs depending on frame material

EASEN tests all bar stools to BIFMA standards (Business and Institutional Furniture Manufacturers Association) for commercial seating.

What upholstery is most durable for high-traffic bars?

Commercial vinyl is the most durable choice:

  • Durability rating: 100,000+ double rubs (Wyzenbeek test)
  • Maintenance: Wipe-clean with damp cloth and mild soap
  • Spill-proof: Non-porous surface resists alcohol, food, and liquid stains
  • Options: 500+ colors, patterns, textures (including leather-look vinyl)
  • Lifespan: 7-10 years in high-traffic bars

Performance fabrics (e.g., Crypton, Revolution) are the next most durable option — stain-resistant and cleanable, but not as bulletproof as vinyl.

Should bar tables have a footrest?

Bar-height stools must have a footrest — it's essential for comfort at 28-30" seat height. Without a footrest, legs dangle uncomfortably.

Footrest options:

  • Built-in footrest: Part of stool frame (most common)
  • Footrest ring: Metal ring around pedestal base of bar tables (alternative when using backless stools)

How do I calculate how many bar stools I need?

Rule of thumb: Allow 24-30" of bar length per stool

  • 24" spacing: Tight/efficient — use for backless stools in crowded bars
  • 26-28" spacing: Standard comfortable spacing
  • 30" spacing: Generous — use for large stools or upscale venues

Example: A 20-foot bar (240" long) can fit 8-10 bar stools depending on spacing.
