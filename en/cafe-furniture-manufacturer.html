Table Top Materials

1. High-Pressure Laminate (HPL)

Most popular for cafes — affordable, durable, and low-maintenance.

  • Construction: Plywood or MDF core + HPL surface + PVC edge banding
  • Appearance: 100+ wood grains, solid colors, marble/concrete patterns
  • Durability: Heat-resistant to 275°F, scratch-resistant, easy to wipe clean
  • Lifespan: 5-7 years in high-traffic cafes
  • Cost: Most economical option

2. Solid Wood

Natural, warm, and upscale — ideal for specialty coffee shops and artisanal cafes.

  • Wood species: Oak, walnut, ash, beech, reclaimed wood
  • Finish: Natural oil, stain + varnish, distressed/rustic
  • Maintenance: Requires periodic re-oiling or refinishing
  • Lifespan: 10-15 years; can be refinished
  • Cost: Premium option

3. Marble & Stone

Luxurious and statement-making — often used for feature tables or high-end cafes.

  • Materials: Natural marble, granite, engineered quartz
  • Appearance: Unique veining, high-end aesthetic
  • Weight: Very heavy — requires robust bases
  • Maintenance: Requires sealing (marble is porous and stains easily)
  • Cost: Premium to luxury

4. Compact Laminate (Outdoor)

Weather-resistant for outdoor cafe seating.

  • Construction: 12-13mm solid laminate (no wood core)
  • Durability: UV-stable, waterproof, won't warp or swell
  • Appearance: Wood grains, solid colors
  • Best for: Sidewalk cafes, patio seating, all-weather use

Outdoor furniture indoors: Yes, no problem. Outdoor-rated furniture is often used indoors for industrial/urban aesthetics.

What's the best cafe table size for maximizing capacity?

For maximum capacity while maintaining comfort:

  • 30"x30" square tables are the most space-efficient for two-person seating
  • Allow 6' x 6' per table (including chair space)
  • Mix in some 24"x24" tables for solo guests (laptop users, quick visits)
  • Add one communal table (30"x60" or longer) to increase capacity without adding many individual tables
  • Use banquette seating along walls to maximize perimeter capacity

Example: A 1,000 sq ft cafe can comfortably seat 35-45 guests with a smart furniture mix.

How do I maintain cafe furniture to extend its lifespan?

Wood furniture:

  • Wipe spills immediately to prevent staining
  • Use coasters to protect from water rings
  • Re-oil or touch up finish annually (for natural wood finishes)
  • Avoid harsh chemical cleaners

Metal furniture:

  • Wipe clean with damp cloth and mild soap
  • Inspect powder-coat for chips; touch up immediately to prevent rust
  • For outdoor furniture, bring indoors during winter if possible

Upholstered furniture:

  • Vacuum weekly with upholstery attachment
  • Spot-clean spills immediately with fabric cleaner
  • Professional steam cleaning every 6-12 months
