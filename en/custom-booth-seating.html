2. Upholstery Materials

Commercial Vinyl

Most durable for restaurants — easy-clean, spill-proof, long lifespan.

  • Durability rating: 100,000-500,000 double rubs (Wyzenbeek)
  • Options: 500+ colors, textures (smooth, leather-look, fabric-look, pebbled)
  • Cleaning: Wipe with damp cloth and mild soap
  • Fire rating: CAL117, NFPA 260 compliant
  • Lifespan: 7-10 years in high-traffic restaurants

Performance Fabric

Upscale textile look with stain resistance — for fine dining and hospitality.

  • Brands: Crypton, Revolution, Sunbrella (indoor), Momentum, etc.
  • Durability: 50,000-100,000 double rubs
  • Stain resistance: Liquid-repellent finish or solution-dyed fibers
  • Cleaning: Spot-clean or professional steam cleaning
  • Best for: Fine dining, hotel restaurants, upscale concepts

Genuine Leather

Premium natural material — luxurious feel and develops patina over time.

  • Grades: Top grain (most common), full grain (premium), corrected grain (budget)
  • Durability: Very durable; ages beautifully with proper care
  • Maintenance: Periodic conditioning with leather care products
  • Cost: Premium option (2-3x vinyl cost)
  • Best for: Steakhouses, upscale dining, hotel signature restaurants

Mixed Materials

Combine materials for visual interest and cost optimization.

  • Example 1: Vinyl seat (high-contact area) + fabric back (decorative)
  • Example 2: Leather front panels + vinyl sides (cost savings)
  • Example 3: Contrasting colors (e.g., black seat + burgundy back)
  • Benefit: Optimize durability and cost while achieving desired aesthetic

3. Tufting & Detailing Styles

Tufting creates visual texture and reinforces the upholstery structure:

Button Tufting (Diamond Pattern)

  • Classic upholstered look
  • Buttons create diamond or square grid pattern
  • Buttons can match or contrast upholstery color
  • Best for: Traditional diners, classic steakhouses, vintage concepts

Channel Tufting (Vertical)

  • Modern, sleek aesthetic
  • Vertical channels create linear visual
  • No buttons (cleaner look)
  • Best for: Contemporary restaurants, modern bistros, hotel dining

Biscuit Tufting

  • Deep, plush squares or rectangles
  • Very luxurious, soft appearance
  • More expensive (complex construction)
  • Best for: Fine dining, upscale lounges, boutique hotels

Plain (No Tufting)

  • Clean, minimalist look
  • Smooth upholstered surface
  • Easiest to clean
  • Best for: Fast-casual, modern cafes, minimalist concepts

4. Wood Trim & Accents

Wood cap rails and trim add warmth and protect upholstery edges:

  • Wood species: Oak, walnut, ash, beech, maple, or veneer options
  • Finish: Natural stain, painted, distressed, ebony, whitewash, custom color-match
  • Cap rail profile: Rounded edge, flat top, bullnose, custom profiles
  • Trim locations: Top of backrest (cap rail), base trim, side panels, dividers
  • Metal accents: Alternative to wood — stainless steel, brass, bronze trim

Upholstery Color & Pattern Selection

Choosing the right upholstery color is critical to your restaurant's aesthetic and long-term maintenance:

Color Strategy by Restaurant Type

Family Restaurants & Diners

  • Recommended: Medium to dark colors; patterns (hides stains)
  • Popular choices: Burgundy, forest green, navy, two-tone patterns
  • Avoid: White, cream, light pastels (show every stain)

Fast-Casual & QSR

  • Recommended: Bold brand colors, easy-clean vinyl
  • Popular choices: Bright red, orange, teal, brand-matched colors
  • Material: Smooth vinyl (wipe-clean is priority)

Fine Dining

  • Recommended: Rich, sophisticated colors; leather or premium fabrics
  • Popular choices: Deep burgundy leather, charcoal, navy, forest green
  • Avoid: Bright colors; high-contrast patterns (too casual)

Cafes & Bistros

  • Recommended: Warm, inviting colors; modern fabrics
  • Popular choices: Mustard yellow, burnt orange, teal, blush, sage
  • Trend: Velvet-look performance fabrics in jewel tones

Maintenance & Stain Resistance by Color

Color Category Stain Visibility Recommended For Avoid If
White / Cream / Light Beige Very high — shows all stains Adult-only fine dining, low-volume cafes Family restaurants, high-volume diners
Medium Neutrals (Taupe, Gray) Moderate — some stains visible Modern cafes, bistros, hotel restaurants BBQ restaurants, very messy cuisines
Dark Colors (Burgundy, Navy, Black) Low — hides most stains well Family restaurants, diners, steakhouses Modern cafes (can feel heavy)
Patterns (Two-Tone, Geometric) Very low — stains blend into pattern Diners, family restaurants, casual dining Fine dining (too busy), minimalist concepts
Bright Colors (Red, Teal, Mustard) Low to moderate — depends on color Fast-casual, cafes, branded chains Fine dining, traditional steakhouses

Standard MOQ: 100 linear feet of booth seating (approximately 4-6 booth sections depending on configuration)

Example:

How do I measure my space for custom booths?

For wall-mounted banquettes:

  1. Measure wall length where booth will be installed
  2. Note locations of outlets, vents, thermostats, wall sconces
  3. Measure ceiling height (for tall backrests)
  4. Photograph the wall from multiple angles

For back-to-back booths:

  1. Measure floor space available (length x width)
  2. Measure distance to nearest obstacles (columns, walkways)
  3. Note floor condition (level? carpeted? tile?)
  4. Consider aisle space (minimum 36" between booth backs and other furniture)

Tip: Send us your floor plan PDF — we'll advise on optimal booth sizing and layout

What if my walls are not perfectly straight?

Not a problem — most walls have some irregularity. Solutions:

  • Flexible back panels: We can build booths with slightly flexible backing that conforms to minor wall curves
  • Scribing strips: Compressible edge trim fills gaps between booth and wall
  • Floating installation: Mount booth slightly away from wall with small gap covered by wood trim
  • Custom shaping: For major curves or angles, we custom-build the back panel to match your wall profile (provide precise measurements)

Can booth cushions be replaced in the future?

Yes. EASEN designs booths with replaceable cushions:

  • Seat cushions: Attached with Velcro or snaps — easily removable for replacement or deep cleaning
  • Back cushions: Can be re-upholstered on-site or removed and shipped back for recovering
  • Replacement service: We retain production records for 5 years and can manufacture replacement cushions matching original specifications
  • Cost: Replacement seat cushions typically $30-60 each depending on size and material

How long do custom booths last?

Frame lifespan: 15-20 years (hardwood frame with proper construction will outlast upholstery)
Upholstery lifespan: 7-10 years for commercial vinyl in high-traffic restaurants; 10-15 years for leather or premium fabrics in lower-volume fine dining

Extending lifespan:

  • Use commercial-grade vinyl (100,000+ double rubs)
  • Choose dark or patterned colors (hide wear better)
  • Clean spills immediately
  • Re-upholster rather than replace entire booth when upholstery wears out
