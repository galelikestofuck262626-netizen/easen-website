Contract Hotel Furniture from Foshan, China

Hotel furniture must balance durability, comfort, aesthetics, and budget across hundreds of identical guest rooms and public spaces. Unlike residential furniture, every piece must withstand years of daily guest use, frequent housekeeping, and rigorous safety standards — all while maintaining the consistent quality and appearance that defines your brand.

Guest Room Furniture

The centerpiece of every guest room:

  • Upholstered headboards: Padded fabric, vinyl, or leather; wall-mounted or bed-attached; heights from 48-72 inches
  • Wood headboards: Solid wood or veneer; panel, slat, or carved designs
  • Bed frames: Platform beds, traditional box spring frames; king, queen, full, twin sizes
  • Floating headboard walls: Full wall upholstery with integrated nightstands and lighting

Key features: Fire-retardant upholstery (CA117), stain-resistant treatments, reinforced corner blocks, easy-access legs for housekeeping

Nightstands & Bedside Tables

Essential storage and surface space:

  • Case goods: Wood or veneer construction; 1-3 drawers

Hotel Furniture Quality Standards

EASEN's hotel furniture is engineered to meet the rigorous demands of commercial hospitality environments:

Durability & Construction

  • Case goods construction: Solid wood frames + plywood or veneer panels (no particleboard in structural areas); dovetail drawer joinery; commercial-grade drawer slides rated for 75,000+ cycles
  • Upholstered furniture: Kiln-dried hardwood frames; 8-way hand-tied springs or sinuous springs; high-density foam (2.0-2.5 lbs/ft³); double-stitched seams with bonded polyester thread
  • Weight capacity: Beds tested to 500 lbs dynamic load; chairs to 300 lbs; sofas to 750 lbs
  • Expected lifespan: 7-12 years in full-service hotels with 70-80% occupancy

Fire Safety & Code Compliance

Hotel furniture must meet stringent fire safety regulations:

  • CAL TB117-2013: California flammability standard for upholstered furniture (required in most U.S. states)
  • NFPA 260: Cigarette ignition resistance (National Fire Protection Association standard)
  • UK BS 7176: British Standard for contract furniture fire resistance (for UK/EU projects)
  • ASTM E84: Surface burning characteristics (flame spread rating)

EASEN provides fire certificates and test reports for upholstered furniture upon request.

Environmental & Safety Certifications

  • CARB Phase 2 compliance: Formaldehyde emission standards for composite wood products
  • FSC-certified wood: Available for environmentally conscious projects
  • Low-VOC finishes: Water-based finishes and adhesives minimize indoor air quality impact
  • GREENGUARD certification: Available for projects pursuing LEED certification

Finish Durability

  • Wood finishes: Catalyzed conversion varnish (superior to standard polyurethane) — resistant to scratches, alcohol, water rings, and cleaning chemicals
  • Veneer quality: Uniform grain matching, tight seams, no telegraphing (underlying substrate showing through)
  • Hardware: Commercial-grade hinges, drawer slides, locks; finishes resistant to tarnishing and corrosion

Cleanability & Maintenance

Hotel furniture must withstand daily housekeeping without degrading:

  • Upholstery: Stain-resistant treatments (Scotchgard™ or similar); cleanable with commercial disinfectants
  • Hard surfaces: Non-porous finishes that won't trap bacteria; resistant to bleach-based cleaners
  • Replaceable components: Drawer pulls, upholstered panels, and cushions designed for easy replacement

Hotel Furniture Project Process

EASEN's structured process ensures on-time, on-budget delivery for hotel projects of all sizes:

Phase 1: Project Consultation (Week 1-2)

  • Review hotel concept, brand standards, room count, and budget
  • Receive floor plans, interior design renderings, and specifications
  • Clarify special requirements (fire ratings, accessibility, certifications)
  • Preliminary furniture list and budget estimate

Phase 2: Design Development (Week 3-5)

  • Detailed shop drawings for each furniture piece
  • Material and finish selection with physical samples
  • 3D renderings showing furniture in actual rooms
  • Final detailed quotation with itemized pricing

Phase 3: Sampling (Week 6-9)

  • Pre-production samples of 3-5 key pieces
  • Client review and approval (on-site or shipped to client)
  • Adjustments based on feedback
  • Final material and color confirmation

Phase 4: Production (Week 10-18)

  • Full production of all furniture (8-12 weeks typical)
  • Weekly progress photos and updates
  • Quality inspections at frame, finishing, and upholstery stages
  • Third-party QC inspection available

Phase 5: Pre-Shipment Inspection (Week 18-19)

  • Final QC inspection of all furniture
  • Dimensional accuracy check
  • Finish quality verification
  • Detailed photo documentation for client approval

Phase 6: Shipping & Installation (Week 20-26)

  • Professional export packing and containerization
  • Sea freight to client's port (3-6 weeks depending on destination)
  • Customs clearance support and documentation
  • Installation coordination (via client's contractor or EASEN technicians)

Total Project Timeline

Small projects (20-50 rooms): 18-22 weeks from order to delivery
Medium projects (50-150 rooms): 20-26 weeks from order to delivery
Large projects (150+ rooms): 24-30 weeks, possibly phased shipments
Rush projects: 14-16 week expedited production available (surcharge applies, limited to certain designs)

Hotel Furniture FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for a hotel furniture project?

There is no strict MOQ for complete hotel projects. We've manufactured furniture for projects as small as 20 guest rooms. For individual furniture pieces ordered separately (not part of a hotel package), MOQs typically range from 20-50 units per item.

Can you manufacture furniture to match our interior designer's specifications?

Yes. EASEN specializes in custom manufacturing to designer specifications. Send us:

  • CAD drawings or dimensioned sketches
  • Material and finish specifications
  • Reference photos or renderings
  • Any special requirements (fire ratings, certifications, etc.)

Our engineering team will review for manufacturability and provide shop drawings for approval before production.

Do you provide fire certificates for upholstered furniture?

Yes. We provide fire test certificates for:

  • CAL TB117-2013 (California)
  • NFPA 260 (USA)
  • UK BS 7176 (UK and Europe)
  • AS/NZS 3837 (Australia/New Zealand)

Specify your jurisdiction's requirements and we'll ensure compliance with proper documentation.

Can you handle phased delivery for a large hotel project?

Yes. For large projects or staggered construction timelines, we can manufacture and ship in phases. For example:

  • Phase 1: Floors 1-5 (100 rooms) — Month 1
  • Phase 2: Floors 6-10 (100 rooms) — Month 3
  • Phase 3: Public areas & F&B — Month 4

This prevents storage bottlenecks on-site and aligns furniture delivery with construction progress.

What warranty do you provide?

EASEN provides a 2-year commercial warranty for hotel furniture projects, covering:

  • Structural failures (frame breakage, joint separation, drawer slide failure)
  • Finish defects (peeling, bubbling, delamination)
  • Manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship

Warranty excludes normal wear and tear, misuse, and damage from improper cleaning. For warranty claims, we provide replacement furniture or parts shipped at our expense.

How is hotel furniture packed for international shipping?

Professional hospitality-grade packing protects furniture during ocean freight:

  • Disassembly: Furniture partially disassembled when practical (legs removed from tables, headboards packed separately from frames)
  • Protection: Individual pieces wrapped in foam corner guards, then bubble wrap, then shrink-wrap
  • Cartons: Heavy-duty export cartons with internal bracing
  • Room-by-room packing: Each guest room's furniture can be packed together with clear labeling for easy installation
  • Container loading: Professional loading to maximize space utilization and prevent shifting

Do you provide installation services?

EASEN provides installation coordination but typically does not perform on-site installation ourselves. Options include:

  • Client's contractor: Most common — your general contractor or FF&E installer handles placement
  • Local installer: We recommend qualified furniture installers in your region
  • EASEN supervision: For complex projects, we can send our technician to supervise installation (travel costs apply)
  • Installation manuals: Detailed assembly and installation instructions provided with every shipment

Can you source other FF&E items beyond furniture?

Yes. While furniture is our core expertise, we can source related hospitality FF&E through our Foshan network:

  • Mattresses and box springs
  • Window treatments (curtains, sheers, blackout systems)
  • Lighting fixtures (table lamps, floor lamps, chandeliers)
  • Mirrors and artwork
  • Rugs and carpets

This provides the convenience of consolidated shipping and single-source coordination.

How do you ensure furniture consistency across hundreds of rooms?

Consistency is critical for hotel projects. EASEN maintains it through:

  • Standardized production: All furniture for a project made in a single production run using identical materials
  • Material bulk purchasing: All wood veneer, fabric, and finishes from the same lot/dye batch
  • Quality templates: First-article samples retained as quality reference for entire production
  • Batch QC: Random inspection of every 20th piece to verify dimensional and finish consistency

What payment terms do you offer for hotel projects?

Standard terms for hotel projects:

  • Deposit: 30% upon order confirmation
  • Progress payment: 40% upon completion of pre-production samples or mid-production (depending on project size)
  • Balance: 30% before shipping

For established clients or large projects, we offer flexible terms including deferred payment or Letter of Credit (L/C) arrangements.
