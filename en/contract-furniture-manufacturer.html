What is Contract Furniture?

Contract furniture refers to furniture manufactured for commercial and institutional use rather than residential consumers. The term "contract" comes from the practice of purchasing furniture through contracts for large-scale projects such as hotels, restaurants, healthcare facilities, senior living communities, corporate offices, and educational institutions.

Contract furniture differs from residential furniture in several critical ways:

  • Commercial-grade construction: Reinforced frames, heavy-duty joinery, and high-density foam designed for constant daily use by multiple users
  • Compliance standards: Must meet fire safety codes (CAL117, NFPA 260, BS 7176), accessibility requirements (ADA), and industry-specific regulations
  • Large-scale procurement: Projects typically involve 50-5,000+ pieces of furniture manufactured and delivered on tight timelines
  • Customization: Furniture tailored to brand standards, space constraints, and functional requirements
  • Project management: Coordinated manufacturing, phased delivery, installation support, and warranty service

Contract Furniture Project Process

EASEN's systematic 6-phase process ensures on-time, on-budget delivery:

Phase 1: Project Inquiry & Assessment

Timeline: 1-5 days

Activities:

  • Review project scope (number of rooms/seats, timeline, budget)
  • Assess design specifications or interior design drawings
  • Clarify compliance requirements (brand standards, fire codes, certifications)
  • Provide preliminary budget estimate
  • Assign dedicated project manager

Phase 2: Design Development & Sampling

Timeline: 3-6 weeks

Activities:

  • Develop detailed shop drawings with dimensions and specifications
  • Present material options (fabrics, finishes, hardware)
  • Manufacture pre-production samples (furniture prototypes)
  • Submit samples for client approval
  • Refine designs based on feedback
  • Finalize pricing and contract

Phase 3: Production Planning

Timeline: 1-2 weeks

Activities:

  • Confirm production schedule aligned with project timeline
  • Procure materials (wood, fabric, hardware)
  • Coordinate phased production if multiple deliveries required
  • Establish quality control checkpoints
  • Arrange testing/certification if needed

Phase 4: Manufacturing

Timeline: 8-12 weeks (depending on project size)

Activities:

  • Frame construction and joinery
  • Finishing (staining, painting, lacquering)
  • Foam cutting and upholstery
  • Assembly and hardware installation
  • In-process quality inspections
  • Weekly production photo updates to client

Phase 5: Quality Control & Packing

Timeline: 1-2 weeks

Activities:

  • Final quality inspection (100% inspection for critical items)
  • Third-party inspection (optional, arranged by client)
  • Room-by-room or location-by-location packing
  • Protective packaging for ocean freight
  • Packing list and installation documentation

Phase 6: Logistics & Delivery

Timeline: 4-8 weeks (sea freight) or 1-2 weeks (air)

Activities:

  • Container loading and export customs clearance
  • Ocean or air freight to destination
  • Import customs clearance and duties
  • Delivery to site (port-to-door or door-to-door)
  • Installation support and final inspection

Do you provide fire safety certifications?

Yes. EASEN provides furniture that meets required fire safety standards and can arrange third-party testing:

  • CAL117-2013 (California TB 117): USA upholstered furniture standard
  • NFPA 260: USA cigarette ignition resistance for furniture
  • BS 7176 (Crib 5): UK contract furniture fire safety
  • ASTM E84 Class A: Flame spread rating for wood finishes and fabrics

Testing is arranged through accredited labs (Intertek, SGS, Bureau Veritas) at your expense (typically $500-2,000 per test). Test reports provided with furniture shipment.

What about import duties and taxes?

Import duties vary by country and furniture type:

  • USA: 0-5% for most furniture (wood chairs/tables 0%, upholstered furniture 0-5.3%)
  • Canada: 0-9.5% depending on materials
  • European Union: 0-3.7% average
  • Australia: 5-10% depending on category
  • Middle East (UAE, Saudi): 5-15%

We provide correct HS codes and documentation for customs clearance. You can work with a customs broker in your country to handle import procedures.

Can you handle phased delivery for hotel projects?

Yes. For hotel projects, we typically deliver furniture in phases aligned with your construction schedule:

  • Phase 1: Public area furniture (lobby, restaurant, meeting rooms) — delivered first for early completion
  • Phase 2-N: Guest room furniture by floor or wing — multiple deliveries as rooms are ready for installation
  • Warehousing option: Store all furniture and release by phase (warehousing fees apply)

Each phase is packed separately with clear labeling for efficient installation.
