Types of Restaurant Chairs We Manufacture

EASEN produces a comprehensive range of commercial dining chairs to suit every restaurant concept, from fast-casual to fine dining:

Typical MOQ: 100 chairs per style

3. Upholstered Restaurant Chairs

Maximum comfort and customizable appearance. Upholstered seats and/or backs elevate the dining experience.

Typical MOQ: 50 chairs per style

4. Stacking Restaurant Chairs

Space-efficient design allows vertical stacking for storage. Essential for venues with flexible seating needs.

Features:

  • Interlocking stack design (8-12 chairs per stack)

  • Lightweight construction (5-10 lbs per chair)

Typical MOQ: 100 chairs per style

5. Bar Stools & Counter Chairs

Elevated seating for bar-height and counter-height tables. Available in wood, metal, and upholstered styles.

Configurations:

  • Counter height (24-26 inch seat)
  • Bar height (28-30 inch seat)

Best for: Bars, brewpubs, sports bars, counter-service restaurants

Typical MOQ: 50-100 stools per style

6. Armchairs & Captain's Chairs

Premium seating with armrests for enhanced comfort. Creates a high-end look and feel.

Features:

  • Wood or upholstered armrests

Typical MOQ: 50 chairs per style

Restaurant Chair Materials & Construction

Material selection profoundly affects a chair's durability, aesthetics, maintenance requirements, and cost. Here's a comprehensive breakdown:

Wood Materials

EASEN works with a variety of hardwoods and wood composites, each with distinct characteristics:

Wood Species Characteristics Durability Cost Best Applications
Beech Hard, fine grain, excellent for bending Very high $$ Classic cafe chairs, bentwood designs, commercial dining
Oak Heavy, prominent grain, traditional look Very high $$$ Upscale restaurants, rustic/farmhouse style, steakhouses
Ash Strong, flexible, light color High $$ Modern/Scandinavian designs, casual dining, cafes
Walnut Rich dark color, straight grain, premium appearance High $$$$ Fine dining, hotel restaurants, executive spaces
Rubber Wood Light color, good stability, economical Medium-high $ Painted finishes, budget-conscious projects, high-volume orders
Plywood (high-grade) Consistent quality, no warping, moldable Medium-high $$ Modern designs, molded plywood shells, laminated/veneer finishes

Metal Materials

Metal chairs offer industrial aesthetics and exceptional durability:

Steel (16-18 gauge)

  • Strength: Extremely strong, 400+ lb capacity
  • Finish: Powder-coat (300+ color options)
  • Weight: Heavy (10-15 lbs per chair)
  • Cost: Moderate
  • Best for: Industrial interiors, high-traffic areas

Aluminum

  • Strength: Strong but lighter than steel
  • Finish: Anodized or powder-coat
  • Weight: Lightweight (5-8 lbs per chair)
  • Cost: Higher than steel
  • Best for: Outdoor patios, modern interiors, stacking chairs

Wrought Iron (decorative)

  • Strength: Very strong, decorative designs possible
  • Finish: Powder-coat or painted
  • Weight: Very heavy (15-25 lbs per chair)
  • Cost: Higher (labor-intensive)
  • Best for: Outdoor cafes, vintage/classic themes, bistros

Brass/Bronze Accents

  • Strength: Decorative, not structural
  • Finish: Polished, brushed, or antiqued
  • Weight: Varies
  • Cost: High (premium material)
  • Best for: Luxury restaurants, art deco designs, accents on wood/upholstered chairs

Upholstery Materials

Upholstery adds comfort and visual appeal but requires careful material selection for commercial durability:

1. Commercial Vinyl

  • Durability: 500,000+ double rubs (Wyzenbeek), ideal for restaurants
  • Cleanability: Excellent — wipe clean with disinfectant
  • Appearance: Available in leather-look, smooth, grain, and textured patterns
  • Flame rating: CA117, NFPA 260 compliant
  • Cost: $8-15 per yard
  • Best for: Casual dining, family restaurants, fast-casual, diners

2. Genuine Leather

  • Durability: Excellent — develops patina with age
  • Cleanability: Requires leather-specific care products
  • Appearance: Luxurious, natural texture, authentic feel
  • Cost: $25-60+ per square foot
  • Best for: Fine dining, steakhouses, upscale hotel restaurants, wine bars

3. Performance Fabric

  • Durability: 100,000+ double rubs for commercial grades
  • Cleanability: Stain-resistant treatment, but not as easy as vinyl
  • Appearance: Soft texture, wide variety of colors/patterns, designer appeal
  • Cost: $15-35 per yard
  • Best for: Boutique restaurants, cafes, hotels, modern interiors

Commercial Chair Specifications & Standards

EASEN's restaurant chairs are engineered to meet or exceed commercial furniture standards:

Weight Capacity

All EASEN restaurant chairs are tested to support:

  • Standard dining chairs: 300-350 lbs static load, 250 lbs dynamic load
  • Heavy-duty chairs: 400-500 lbs static load (bariatric-rated available)
  • Stacking chairs: 250-300 lbs per chair, stacks of 8-12 chairs safely supported

Testing & Certifications

Upon request, EASEN can provide:

  • BIFMA (Business and Institutional Furniture Manufacturers Association) testing: Comprehensive durability, stability, and safety testing to ANSI/BIFMA X5.1 and X5.4 standards
  • CAL 117-2013 flame retardancy: Required for upholstered furniture sold in California
  • NFPA 260: National Fire Protection Association cigarette ignition standard
  • EN 1022 / EN 16139: European standards for contract furniture (for EU exports)

Joinery & Construction Quality

What separates commercial-grade chairs from residential furniture:

  • Mortise-and-tenon joinery: Traditional strongest joint for wood chairs
  • Corner blocks: Triangular wood or metal blocks reinforce seat-to-leg joints
  • Glued-and-screwed construction: Both glue and mechanical fasteners for redundant strength
  • Full-perimeter seat frames: Four-sided seat frame (not just two rails) for maximum strength
  • Metal-to-metal welding (metal chairs): Full-penetration welds, ground smooth

Finish Durability

  • Wood finish: Catalyzed conversion varnish — superior scratch, chemical, and moisture resistance vs. standard polyurethane
  • Powder-coat finish (metal): 2-3 mils thick, 200+ hour salt spray corrosion resistance, impact resistant
  • Upholstery seams: Double-stitched with bonded polyester thread (UV-resistant, high tensile strength)

Can you match chairs to a specific design or competitor's product?

Yes. Send us clear photos from multiple angles, dimensions (or a physical sample if possible), and material specifications. Our team will reverse-engineer the design and provide a matching quotation. We can replicate:

  • Overall dimensions and proportions
  • Wood species and stain color
  • Metal finish and color
  • Upholstery material and color (we'll send swatches for approval)

Note: We cannot replicate patented designs or designs where the original manufacturer holds exclusive rights.

How are restaurant chairs packed for international shipping?

Professional export packing protects chairs during ocean freight:

  • Individual protection: Each chair wrapped in bubble wrap or foam, then shrink-wrapped
  • Carton packing: Chairs nested (stacked seat-to-seat) in sturdy corrugated cartons
  • Carton capacity: Typically 4-8 chairs per carton depending on size
  • Palletization: Cartons palletized and secured with strapping for container loading
  • Container utilization: We optimize packing to maximize pieces per container, reducing your per-chair shipping cost

Example: A standard wood dining chair (17" x 18" x 35") packed 6 per carton yields approximately 1,200-1,500 chairs per 40' HC container.

Do restaurant chairs require assembly?

Most restaurant chairs ship fully assembled and ready to use upon unpacking. This applies to:

  • Standard wood dining chairs
  • Metal chairs (all-welded construction)
  • Fully upholstered chairs

Some chair types may require minimal assembly to save shipping space:

  • Chairs with removable seat cushions: Cushions may be packed separately and attached on-site with Velcro or ties
  • Certain metal chairs with bolt-on seats: Seats attach with 4 screws (takes 2 minutes per chair, tools included)

Assembly requirements are always specified in the quotation.

What's the expected lifespan of commercial restaurant chairs?

Lifespan depends on traffic level, maintenance, and construction quality:

  • High-traffic fast-casual (500+ covers/day): 3-5 years
  • Moderate-traffic casual dining (200-400 covers/day): 5-8 years
  • Low-traffic fine dining (50-150 covers/day): 10-15 years

EASEN's commercial-grade construction extends lifespan by 30-50% compared to residential-grade chairs used in commercial settings. Wood chairs tend to last longer than upholstered chairs (upholstery typically needs replacement or reupholstering after 5-7 years in high-traffic restaurants).

Can chairs be repaired if damaged?

Yes. Most damage is repairable:

  • Loose joints: Re-glue and clamp (or add corner blocks if not originally present)
  • Scratched wood finish: Touch-up markers or refinishing
  • Damaged upholstery: Reupholster seat and/or back (EASEN can provide replacement upholstery kits with pre-cut foam and fabric)
  • Broken legs or back slats: Replacement parts available for standard designs

For warranty claims (manufacturing defects within 2 years), we provide replacement parts or chairs free of charge.

Do you offer flame-retardant options for upholstered chairs?

Yes. All upholstered chairs can be manufactured with:

  • FR foam: Meets CA117-2013 requirements
  • FR fabric/vinyl: Inherently flame-retardant materials or FR-treated fabrics
  • FR barrier cloth: Additional layer between foam and upholstery for extra protection

Specify your jurisdiction's requirements (California, UK, EU, etc.) and we'll ensure compliance. FR certification documentation provided upon request.

Can I order replacement chairs years later to match my existing set?

Yes, with some considerations:

  • Within 3 years: We typically retain production records and can manufacture exact matches (same wood stain, upholstery, finish)
  • After 3-5 years: Material availability may change (upholstery patterns discontinued, wood stain formulas updated). We'll get as close as possible and send samples for approval.
  • Standard designs: Easier to match long-term vs. highly customized one-off designs

Tip: If you anticipate needing replacements, we recommend ordering 10-15% extra chairs upfront to keep as spares. This guarantees perfect color/finish matching.

What warranty do you provide?

EASEN provides a 2-year commercial warranty covering:

  • Structural failures (broken frames, joint failure)
  • Finish defects (peeling, blistering, delamination)
  • Manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship

Warranty does not cover:

  • Normal wear and tear (upholstery fading, wood scratches from use)
  • Damage from misuse, vandalism, or accidents
  • Modifications made by third parties

Warranty claims: We provide replacement chairs or parts shipped free of charge. For large claims, we may arrange inspection via third-party QC company.
