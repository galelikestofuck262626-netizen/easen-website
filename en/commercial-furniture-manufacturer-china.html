About EASEN Commercial Furniture

Founded in Foshan, Guangdong Province — the heart of China's furniture manufacturing industry — EASEN Furniture has been serving the global commercial furniture market since 2016. We specialize in custom-designed, contract-grade furniture for hospitality, foodservice, and commercial interiors.

Why Choose EASEN as Your Commercial Furniture Manufacturer?

1. Foshan Manufacturing Hub Advantage

EASEN's factory is located in Foshan, Guangdong Province — China's most concentrated furniture manufacturing region. This strategic location gives us unmatched advantages:

  • Immediate access to specialized suppliers — High-grade timber, commercial-grade foam, contract vinyl, powder-coating services, and hardware all within 30 minutes of our factory
  • Deep talent pool — Foshan has three generations of skilled furniture craftsmen and upholsterers
  • Efficient logistics — Direct highway access to Guangzhou Port (40km) and Shenzhen Port (120km) for fast, cost-effective international shipping
  • Scale economies — Foshan's cluster effect means lower material costs and faster prototyping

2. True Full-Customization Capabilities

Unlike trading companies that offer "customization" within limited templates, EASEN manufactures everything in-house from raw materials. This means genuine design freedom:

  • Custom dimensions — Any size to fit your space perfectly
  • Material selection — Choose from solid wood species (oak, walnut, ash, beech), metal types (steel, aluminum, brass), upholstery grades (commercial vinyl, top-grain leather, performance fabrics), and laminate options
  • Finish customization — Powder-coat colors, wood stains, distressing, metal patinas
  • Design modifications — Adjust proportions, add features (USB ports, power outlets, coat hooks), change construction methods
  • Branding integration — Custom logos, embroidery, laser engraving

3. Contract-Grade Quality Standards

EASEN furniture is engineered for commercial durability, not residential use. Our quality standards include:

  • Frame construction — Kiln-dried hardwood joinery with corner blocks, glued-and-screwed construction, 16-gauge steel tubing for metal frames
  • Foam specifications — High-density commercial-grade foam (minimum 2.2 lbs/ft³ for seats), with reinforced lumbar support
  • Upholstery durability — Commercial-grade vinyl rated for 500,000+ double rubs (Wyzenbeek test), flame retardant to CA117 and UK FMVSS302
  • Finish durability — Catalyzed conversion varnish for wood (superior to polyurethane), powder-coat for metal with 200+ hour salt spray rating
  • Weight capacity — Chairs tested to 400 lbs dynamic load, booths to 350 lbs per linear foot
  • Testing — BIFMA compliance testing available for North American projects

4. Flexible MOQ & Project Sizes

We support projects of all scales:

  • Small boutique projects — Single locations with as few as 20-30 pieces
  • Regional rollouts — 5-10 locations with mixed furniture types
  • National chains — 50+ locations with standardized SKUs and warehousing support
  • Replacement & refresh orders — Replenish worn furniture for existing locations

Unlike larger factories that require 500+ unit MOQs, EASEN's flexible production model allows us to profitably serve smaller clients while still offering competitive pricing on large orders.

Commercial Furniture Manufacturing Process

EASEN's streamlined process takes your project from concept to delivery in 6-10 weeks:

Step 1: Design Consultation

Share your project details, floor plans, design inspiration, and budget. Our team will recommend furniture types, dimensions, and materials based on your concept and traffic expectations.

Timeline: 2-3 days

Step 2: Detailed Quotation

We provide an itemized quote with 3D renderings or CAD drawings showing your furniture in context. Quote includes FOB pricing, estimated shipping, and lead time.

Timeline: 3-5 days

Step 3: Sampling (Optional)

For large projects or first-time clients, we recommend pre-production samples. Test the comfort, quality, and finish before committing to full production.

Timeline: 2-3 weeks

Step 4: Production

Full manufacturing with quality checkpoints at frame construction, finishing, upholstery, and final assembly stages. You receive progress photos weekly.

Timeline: 4-6 weeks

Step 5: Quality Inspection

Every piece is inspected for structural integrity, finish quality, upholstery tension, and dimensional accuracy. Third-party QC inspections available upon request.

Timeline: 2-3 days

Step 6: Shipping & Delivery

Professional packing to prevent damage in transit. We arrange sea freight or air freight to your location, with door-to-door delivery options available.

Timeline: 3-6 weeks (sea), 7-10 days (air)

Total Lead Time

Without sampling: 6-8 weeks from PO to ex-factory
With sampling: 8-10 weeks from PO to ex-factory
Rush orders: Expedited 4-5 week production available for urgent projects (surcharge applies)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Our MOQ varies by product complexity. For standard items like dining chairs, the MOQ is typically 50-100 pieces per SKU. For custom booth seating or upholstered items, we can work with orders as small as 20-30 pieces. Contact us with your project details for a specific MOQ.

Can you manufacture furniture to match our existing pieces?

Yes. Send us photos, dimensions, and (if possible) a physical sample. Our team will reverse-engineer the design and provide a matching quotation. We can match wood species, stain colors, upholstery, and hardware to ensure visual continuity.

Do you offer flame-retardant (FR) upholstery for commercial applications?

Absolutely. All of our upholstered furniture can be manufactured with commercial-grade flame-retardant materials that meet CA117 (California Technical Bulletin 117), NFPA 260, and UK FMVSS302 standards. We work with leading FR fabric and vinyl suppliers to ensure compliance.

What are the payment terms?

Standard terms are 30% deposit upon order confirmation, 70% balance before shipping. We accept wire transfer (T/T), and for established clients, we offer flexible payment terms. Letter of Credit (L/C) is accepted for large orders (typically $50,000+).

How is the furniture packed for international shipping?

We use professional export packing designed to prevent damage during ocean freight:

  • Disassembled when practical to save space and reduce shipping costs
  • Individual pieces wrapped in protective foam, then shrink-wrapped
  • Placed in sturdy corrugated cartons with corner protection
  • Palletized and secured for container loading
  • All hardware and assembly instructions included

We optimize packing to maximize container utilization, reducing your per-piece shipping cost.

Can you ship directly to multiple locations?

Yes. For multi-location rollouts, we can arrange:

  • Split shipments to different addresses from our factory
  • Warehousing in your destination country with scheduled releases
  • Partnership with your existing logistics provider for final-mile delivery

Do you provide assembly instructions?

Yes. Every shipment includes detailed assembly instructions with illustrations. For complex installations (like continuous booth seating), we provide installation guides with step-by-step photos. For very large projects, we can arrange for our technician to travel to your site for installation supervision (travel costs apply).

What is your warranty policy?

EASEN provides a 2-year commercial warranty covering manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship. This includes frame failure, finish defects, and premature upholstery wear under normal commercial use. Warranty does not cover damage from misuse, vandalism, or normal wear-and-tear. Replacement parts ship free of charge during the warranty period.

How do I get started?

Contact us with your project details — location, concept, approximate furniture count, budget, and timeline. We'll schedule a consultation call to discuss your needs and provide a preliminary quotation within 3-5 business days.
