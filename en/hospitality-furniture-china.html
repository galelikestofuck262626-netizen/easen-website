What fire safety certifications do you provide?

EASEN provides furniture meeting these fire safety standards (testing arranged through accredited third-party labs):

  • CAL117-2013 (California TB 117): USA upholstered furniture smoldering combustion standard
  • NFPA 260: USA cigarette ignition resistance for furniture components
  • BS 7176 (Crib 5): UK contract furniture fire safety standard (medium and high hazard)
  • ASTM E84 Class A: Flame spread rating for fabrics and wood finishes
  • EN 1021: European furniture ignitability standard

Testing reports provided with furniture shipment for compliance verification.

Can you handle phased delivery for hotel projects?

Yes. For hotel projects we typically deliver in phases aligned with construction:

  • Phase 1: Public area furniture (lobby, restaurant, meeting rooms) — delivered first for early completion
  • Phase 2-N: Guest room furniture by floor or wing — multiple shipments as rooms are ready

Each phase is packed separately with clear labeling. We can also warehouse completed furniture and release on your schedule (warehousing fees apply).
