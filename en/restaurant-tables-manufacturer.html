Restaurant Table Top Options

The table top defines your restaurant's aesthetic and must be chosen based on durability, maintenance, budget, and style. EASEN offers five primary table top categories:

1. High-Pressure Laminate (HPL)

Most popular for commercial restaurants — combines durability, affordability, and design flexibility.

Construction:

  • Plywood or MDF core (18-25mm thick)
  • HPL surface (0.8-1.2mm thick)
  • Heat-resistant to 275°F
  • Scratch and impact resistant
  • 2-3mm PVC edge banding

Appearance: 100+ wood grains, solid colors, patterns available

Maintenance: Wipe clean with damp cloth and mild cleaner

Lifespan: 5-8 years in high-traffic restaurants

Best for: Casual dining, fast-casual, cafes, diners, family restaurants

2. Solid Wood Table Tops

Classic, authentic, and warm — solid wood tops create an upscale, natural aesthetic.

Construction:

  • Edge-glued solid wood planks
  • 1.5-2 inch (38-50mm) thickness
  • Sealed with catalyzed conversion varnish
  • Wood species: Oak, walnut, ash, beech, acacia

Appearance: Natural wood grain, rich color, authentic texture

Maintenance: Requires periodic re-oiling or refinishing (every 1-3 years)

Lifespan: 10-15 years with proper care; can be refinished

Best for: Fine dining, steakhouses, wine bars, rustic/farmhouse concepts

3. Wood Veneer Table Tops

Real wood appearance at a lower cost than solid wood, with better dimensional stability.

Construction:

  • Compact Laminate (Exterior Grade): Weather-resistant, UV-stable, 12-13mm thick, non-porous surface, won't warp or swell. Ideal for outdoor patios, rooftop terraces.
  • Aluminum Composite: Lightweight, rustproof, powder-coat finish. Best for coastal locations, poolside dining.
  • Marine-Grade Plywood with Teak Veneer: Traditional yacht-quality construction, rich appearance. Premium outdoor option.
  • Solid Recycled Plastic (HDPE): Maintenance-free, looks like wood, never needs sealing. Good for high-UV environments.

    • Appearance: Wood grain patterns, natural color variations

    Maintenance: Easy wipe-clean, more resistant to stains than solid wood

    Lifespan: 7-10 years

    Best for: Upscale casual, hotel restaurants, boutique cafes

    4. Granite & Marble Stone Tops

    Premium, heavy, and luxurious — stone tops make a statement and last decades.

    Construction:

    • Natural granite or marble slab
    • 0.75-1.25 inch (20-30mm) thickness
    • Polished, honed, or leathered finish
    • Sealed for stain resistance

    Appearance: Unique natural veining, high-end look

    Maintenance: Requires periodic re-sealing (annually); marble is porous and stains easily

    Lifespan: 20+ years

    Best for: Fine dining, hotel signature restaurants, bars (popular for bar tops)

    5. Outdoor Table Tops

    Weather-resistant materials designed for patio, terrace, and outdoor dining use.

    Options:

    • Compact Laminate (Exterior Grade): Weather-resistant, UV-stable, 12-13mm thick, non-porous surface, won't warp or swell. Ideal for outdoor patios, rooftop terraces.
    • Aluminum Composite: Lightweight, rustproof, powder-coat finish. Best for coastal locations, poolside dining.
    • Marine-Grade Plywood with Teak Veneer: Traditional yacht-quality construction, rich appearance. Premium outdoor option.
    • Solid Recycled Plastic (HDPE): Maintenance-free, looks like wood, never needs sealing. Good for high-UV environments.

    Best for: Outdoor cafes, rooftop bars, patio dining, resort restaurants

    6. Butcher Block Table Tops

    Thick, durable work-surface style tops — functional and rustic aesthetic.

    Construction:

    • Edge-grain or end-grain construction
    • 1.5-3 inch (38-75mm) thickness
    • Hard maple, oak, or walnut
    • Food-safe mineral oil or varnish finish

    Appearance: Checkerboard end-grain or striped edge-grain pattern

    Maintenance: Requires regular oiling (monthly for end-grain)

    Lifespan: 10-20 years; can be sanded and refinished

    Best for: Gastropubs, farm-to-table restaurants, brewery taprooms

    Restaurant Table Base Options

    The table base determines stability, footprint, and style. EASEN manufactures a full range of commercial-grade table bases:

    Cast Iron Table Bases

    Most popular for commercial restaurants — heavy, stable, durable, and affordable.

    • Column bases: Single central column with round or square cast iron base plate (22-30" diameter). Supports table tops up to 36" round or 30"x48" rectangular.
    • Finish: Black powder-coat (standard), brushed metal, antique finishes available
    • Weight: 40-80 lbs depending on size — provides excellent stability without floor anchoring
    • Height: Standard dining (28-30"), bar height (40-42"), counter height (34-36")
    • Best for: Bistros, cafes, casual dining, indoor and covered outdoor use

    Steel Table Bases

    Modern, industrial aesthetic with welded steel construction.

    • X-base: Two diagonal steel flat bars forming an X. Minimalist industrial look.
    • T-base: Wide rectangular base with single vertical column. Good for rectangular tops.
    • Cruciform (4-prong): Four legs radiating from center column in + shape. Maximum stability.
    • Tubing: 2"x2" or 3"x3" square tubing, 16-gauge steel
    • Finish: Black, white, gray powder-coat; raw steel with clear coat; brushed stainless
    • Best for: Modern cafes, breweries, fast-casual chains, industrial interiors

    Wood Table Bases

    Traditional, warm, and versatile — wood bases suit classic and rustic concepts.

    • Four-leg bases: Classic dining table base with four legs and apron (skirt). Supports large rectangular tops.
    • Trestle bases: Two vertical supports connected by horizontal beam. Farmhouse/rustic style.
    • Pedestal bases: Single central column with tripod or X-shaped foot. Elegant, space-efficient.
    • Wood species: Oak, walnut, ash, beech — matches wood table tops
    • Finish: Stained and sealed with conversion varnish, painted, distressed
    • Best for: Fine dining, farmhouse restaurants, traditional bistros, wine bars

    Stainless Steel Bases

    Premium, sanitary, and corrosion-proof — ideal for high-end and outdoor applications.

    • Material: 304-grade stainless steel (food-service grade)
    • Finish: Brushed (most common), mirror-polished, bead-blasted
    • Styles: Column bases, cruciform bases, flat bar bases
    • Best for: Upscale restaurants, coastal locations (salt-air resistant), outdoor patios, modern interiors

    Folding & Flip-Top Bases

    Space-saving bases for venues with flexible seating needs.

    • Folding leg bases: Legs fold flat against underside of top for stacking and storage
    • Flip-top mechanism: Top flips vertical and locks; base on casters for nesting storage
    • Best for: Banquet halls, conference centers, multi-use spaces, catering

    Table Configurations & Sizing Guide

    Choosing the right table sizes and configurations affects seating capacity, traffic flow, and guest comfort:

    Standard Table Heights

    Type Table Height Chair/Stool Seat Height Applications
    Dining Height 28-30 inches 17-19 inches Standard restaurant dining, cafes, bistros
    Counter Height 34-36 inches 24-26 inches Casual dining, counter seating, bars
    Bar Height 40-42 inches 28-30 inches Bars, pubs, standing-height cocktail areas

    Seating Capacity Guidelines

    How many guests can comfortably fit at each table size?

    • 24"x24" square: 2 guests (tight for 2, better as single-person or laptop tables)
    • 30"x30" square: 2-4 guests (comfortable for 2, tight for 4)
    • 36"x36" square: 4 guests comfortably
    • 24"x42" rectangular: 2-4 guests (2 on long sides, 1 on each end if needed)
    • 30"x48" rectangular: 4-6 guests
    • 36"x60" rectangular: 6-8 guests
    • 30" round: 2-3 guests
    • 36" round: 3-4 guests
    • 42" round: 4-5 guests
    • 48" round: 5-6 guests
    • 60" round: 8-10 guests (common for banquets)

    Note: Allow 24-30 inches of table perimeter per guest for comfortable seating.

    Table Spacing & Traffic Flow

    Proper spacing between tables affects both guest comfort and code compliance:

    • Table edge to table edge: Minimum 18 inches (tight); 24 inches (standard); 30-36 inches (comfortable)
    • Table to wall: Minimum 18 inches for chair pullout; 24 inches for guest passage; 30 inches for server passage with tray
    • Main aisles: 36-48 inches for two-way server traffic with trays
    • Fire code egress: Check local codes — typically 44 inches minimum for primary exit paths

    Restaurant Tables FAQ

    What is the typical lead time for custom restaurant tables?

    Standard production: 5-7 weeks from order confirmation to ex-factory
    With pre-production sample: 2-3 weeks for sample + 5-7 weeks for production = 7-10 weeks total
    Rush orders: 3-4 week production possible for certain configurations (surcharge applies)

    What is the minimum order quantity?

    MOQ varies by table configuration:

    • Laminate tables (standard designs): 20-50 tables per size/finish combination
    • Wood tables: 20-50 tables per wood species/finish
    • Metal base tables: 50 tables per base style/finish
    • Highly customized designs: May require higher MOQ (50-100 units) due to tooling costs

    For multi-location rollouts or repeat orders, we can ship smaller quantities per order while maintaining overall volume commitments.

    Can you match tables to a specific design or competitor's product?

    Yes. Send us clear photos, dimensions, and material specifications. We can replicate:

    • Table top dimensions, shape, and edge profile
    • Laminate pattern or wood species/stain
    • Base style, material, and finish

    We'll provide matching samples for approval before full production.

    How are restaurant tables packed for international shipping?

    Professional export packing protects tables during ocean freight:

    • Disassembly: Table tops and bases ship separately to save space and reduce damage risk
    • Table tops: Wrapped in foam corner protectors, then corrugated cardboard, then shrink-wrapped. Stacked flat on pallets.
    • Bases: Wrapped individually, nested when possible, secured in cartons
    • Container capacity: Example: 30"x30" laminate tops with cast iron bases = approximately 150-200 complete tables per 40' HC container

    Do restaurant tables require assembly?

    Yes, but it's quick and simple. Tables ship with tops and bases separate:

    • Column-base tables: Attach base to underside of top with 3-4 screws (takes 3-5 minutes per table, tools included)
    • Four-leg tables: Attach four legs to corner brackets with bolts (takes 10-15 minutes per table)
    • No specialized skills required: Assembly instructions with photos included with every shipment

    What's the expected lifespan of commercial restaurant tables?

    Lifespan depends on traffic level and maintenance:

    • Laminate tables (high traffic): 5-8 years before laminate shows significant wear
    • Solid wood tables: 10-15 years; can be refinished to extend life
    • Stone tables: 20+ years
    • Cast iron bases: 15-20 years (very durable)
    • Steel bases: 10-15 years (powder-coat may need touch-up after 7-10 years in high-wear areas)

    Can tables be used outdoors?

    Only tables built with outdoor-rated materials:

    • Table tops: Use compact laminate (exterior grade), aluminum composite, marine plywood with teak veneer, or solid HDPE plastic. Standard indoor HPL will delaminate outdoors.
    • Bases: Use stainless steel, powder-coated aluminum, or cast iron with marine-grade powder-coat. Standard black powder-coat on steel will rust.
    • Hardware: All fasteners must be stainless steel (not zinc-plated)

    Specify "outdoor use" when ordering and we'll ensure all components are weather-rated.

    How do I choose the right table base for my table top?

    Base size must be proportional to top size for stability:

    • Round/square tops up to 30": 22" diameter base minimum
    • Round/square tops 32-42": 26-30" diameter base
    • Rectangular tops up to 30"x48": 22"x30" base or 26" round base
    • Rectangular tops 36"x60" or larger: Requires two column bases or four-leg base
    • General rule: Base spread should be at least 50-60% of the table top's smallest dimension

    Send us your desired table top sizes and we'll recommend appropriately sized bases.

    Can I mix and match different table sizes in one order?

    Yes. You can order multiple table sizes in a single shipment, as long as your total order meets our MOQ. For example:

    • 20 tables at 30"x30" (two-tops)
    • 15 tables at 30"x48" (four-tops)
    • 10 tables at 36"x60" (six-tops)

    Total: 45 tables — meets MOQ. All can share the same laminate finish and base style for consistency.

    What warranty do you provide?

    EASEN provides a 2-year commercial warranty covering:

    • Structural failures (base failure, top warping, delamination)
    • Finish defects (peeling powder-coat, laminate edge separation)
    • Manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship

    Warranty does not cover:

    • Normal wear and tear (surface scratches, laminate dulling)
    • Damage from misuse, impacts, or accidents
    • Using indoor-rated tables outdoors

    Warranty claims: We provide replacement tops, bases, or complete tables shipped free of charge.
